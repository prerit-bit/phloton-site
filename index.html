Technology
How Phloton works

A convergence of thermoelectric cooling, IoT, and AI — engineered for extreme environments with zero refrigerants.

Thermoelectric Cooling

Peltier-effect modules with phase change material maintain 2–8°C without any refrigerants or moving parts.

IoT Sensors

Bluetooth-connected temperature and humidity sensors transmit real-time data to a cloud dashboard and mobile app.

AI / ML Analytics

Predictive algorithms optimize power consumption and anticipate temperature excursions before they happen.

Solar + Off-Grid

Solar panel integration and swappable battery packs enable operation in areas with zero electricity infrastructure.
